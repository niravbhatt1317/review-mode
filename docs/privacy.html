Review mode: privacy

Review mode is a browser extension for design teams. It lets a signed-in team member leave comments on web pages they are reviewing. This page says what the extension handles and where it goes. Last updated 23 October 2026.

What the extension collects

  • Your GitHub identity. When you sign in with GitHub, the extension receives your GitHub username, display name, email address and profile picture, and keeps a sign-in session in the browser's extension storage so you stay signed in.
  • Comments and screenshots. When you make a comment on a site you have switched on, the extension saves the comment text, a tag, the address and title of the page, a description of the element you clicked, a screenshot of the visible part of that page at that moment, and the clicks that led there, so the comment can be found again later.
  • Status changes. When a comment is picked up, done, sent back or approved, the extension records who did it and when, and any note typed with it.
  • Which sites are on. The list of sites you switched on and their project names is kept in the browser's extension storage.

What it does not do

  • It does nothing on sites you have not switched on: no reading, no screenshots, no injection.
  • It does not read your browsing history, passwords, form entries or other extensions' data.
  • It does not sell data, show advertising, or send anything to anyone other than your team's own hub.

Where the data goes

Comments, screenshots, statuses and your name and picture are stored in a database and file store operated by your team (a Supabase project the team administers), so your teammates can see and act on them. Sign-in is handled by GitHub and the same Supabase project. Screenshots of approved comments are deleted automatically 14 days after approval; the comment record is kept.

Your choices

  • Sign out at any time from the extension's popup; this removes the session from your browser.
  • Remove any comment you made from the list; its screenshot is deleted with it.
  • Switch a site off from the popup; the extension then stops acting on it.
  • Uninstalling the extension removes everything it kept in the browser.

Contact

Questions about this extension or the data it handles: contact the team that administers your review hub.
